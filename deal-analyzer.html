Questions buyers ask

New to acquisition financing? Start with how to finance buying a business — the three sources of money, and the one number every lender checks first.

What is SDE and why not just use net income?

Seller's discretionary earnings is net income with interest, taxes, depreciation, the owner's full compensation and personal expenses added back. It is what one owner-operator can take out of the business. Listings quote it because it is the largest defensible number; the analyzer recomputes it so you can see which add-backs are real.

What DSCR do SBA lenders want?

Most look for 1.25x or better on historical cash flow after a reasonable salary for you. The calculator above uses 1.25x to solve for your maximum price.

Does a seller note count as my equity injection?

Only if it is on full standby for the term of the SBA loan, and only up to half of the required injection under current rules. Confirm structure with your lender before you sign a letter of intent.

Is the E-2 screen legal advice?

No. It checks whether the numbers fall in a credible range for the substantial, at-risk and non-marginal tests. A licensed immigration attorney makes the call.
